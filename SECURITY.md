# Reporting security issues

anynear is a beta and has not been independently audited. Reports of vulnerabilities are welcome.

- **Report:** by email to <support@anynear.app>. Please **do not open a public issue** for vulnerabilities.
- **Include:** what you observed and how to reproduce it (app version under “About anynear”, device, steps).
- **Do not send:** other people's data, invitation or contact links, backup files, keys.
- **Process:** We confirm receipt and get back to you with an assessment. Please give us time to fix the issue before
  you publish details.
- **Testing:** only with your own devices and your own circles. Please do not disrupt the anynear network (relays) or
  other people's use of it.
- **Recognition:** There is no bounty. If you wish, we credit you once the issue is fixed.

Methods and known limits: <https://anynear.app/de/technik/> (German).
